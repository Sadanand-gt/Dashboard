\# Enterprise UI



Design for:



\- NBFC

\- Banking

\- MIS Reporting

\- Credit Risk

\- Operations



\## Principles



\- Information dense

\- Professional appearance

\- Fast scanning

\- Minimal animation



\## Colors



Primary:



\- Blue

\- Slate

\- Neutral



Avoid:



\- Excessive gradients

\- Gaming-style interfaces

\- Consumer-app aesthetics



\## Layout



Use card-based layouts.



Prioritize readability over decoration.
