\# Dashboard Architect



Always structure dashboards:



1\. KPI Cards

2\. Filters

3\. Charts

4\. Detail Tables



\## KPI Rules



Maximum 4 cards per row.



Include:



\- Current Value

\- Previous Value

\- Variance

\- Trend



\## Charts



Use:



\- Recharts

\- Bar

\- Line

\- Area

\- Donut



Avoid:



\- Pie charts when comparison is important

\- Decorative charts



\## Tables



Use:



\- Sticky headers

\- Sorting

\- Filtering

\- Excel Export

\- Pagination



\## Executive Dashboard Rules



Highlight:



\- Risk

\- Growth

\- Revenue

\- Collections

\- Portfolio Quality
