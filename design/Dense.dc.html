What this buys
Roughly twice the rows per screen: the KPI band folds into the parameter bar and the chart panel becomes an icon. Grouped headers let the eye take "portfolio at risk" as one block. All four PAR columns keep their shading, so any of them can be the one you are hunting on.
The trade-off
Four shaded columns next to each other is a lot of colour, and near-identical PAR values make wide bands of it. The totals rail is less glanceable than cards. At 0.72rem this is a desk report, not something to read on a laptop across a meeting table.
