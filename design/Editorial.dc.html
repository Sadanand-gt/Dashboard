What this buys
The bar answers "how far off is this branch" in one glance and to scale — a fill can only say which of five buckets it landed in. The benchmark is a line you can see, not a number in a legend. Reads well at distance, so it suits a board pack or a screen share.
The trade-off
The bar costs a wide column, so only one measure gets it and fewer columns fit. Rows are tall — about a third of Dense on screen. It exports poorly: a CSV keeps the number and loses the comparison entirely.
